# The final test: a long passage. Maintain accuracy over the whole text, and keep your shoulders relaxed.
Learning to type well is mostly a matter of patience. At
first every key is a small decision, and every word takes
effort. Gradually the decisions fade into habit: the fingers
know where to go, the eyes stay on the text, and the mind is
free to think about what is being said rather than how it is
being typed. That is the real goal of touch typing, and it
arrives sooner than most people expect if they practise a
little every day, keep their accuracy high, and resist the
urge to look down at the keyboard.
